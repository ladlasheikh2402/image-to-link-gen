Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy explains what Ladla Sheikh Image to Link collects, why, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

  • Account details (only if you sign up): name, email, profile photo (when you use Google or GitHub), login method, user ID, and join / last-login time.
  • Upload records: image link, thumbnail, file name, size, dimensions, upload time and delete link. Logged-in users have these saved in their account; guests have them saved only in their own browser.
  • The images themselves, which you choose to upload to the image host.
  • Preferences such as theme and switches, kept in your browser.

2. How we use it

To run the Service: sign you in, show your history, sync it across your devices, and show your statistics. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not build advertising profiles.

3. Services we rely on

  • Google Firebase for sign-in and for storing your account and upload records.
  • Google or GitHub sign-in, only if you choose those buttons.
  • ImgBB for hosting uploaded images.
  • QR code service (api.qrserver.com), which receives your image link to draw its QR code.
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs for fonts and icons, which can see your IP address when loading.

These providers have their own privacy policies. We share data only with them, or when the law requires it.

4. Your choices

  • Download your data: Account, then Download my data.
  • Delete your account and every saved record: Account, then Delete my account & data.
  • Delete a file from the host: use the delete from server link on its card.
  • As a guest, clear your browser storage to remove everything saved on your device.

5. Security and public links

Account access rules are set up so that each user can only read and write their own records. Even so, no online service is 100% secure. Anyone who has an image link can view that image, so do not upload private or sensitive pictures.

6. Retention

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Guest data stays in your browser until you clear it.

7. Children

The Service is not meant for children under 13.
